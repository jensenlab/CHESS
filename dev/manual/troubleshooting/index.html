Troubleshooting

A lookup of the error types CHESSCore raises most often, what triggers each one, and where the concept behind it is covered in detail.

LockedLocationError

Thrown by move_into! when the location being moved is is_locked. Locked locations cannot be moved from their current parent, though their own children can still be moved. See Movement & Occupancy for the full set of move-refusal reasons.

AlreadyLocatedInError

Thrown by move_into! when the location being moved is already inside the target parent. See Movement & Occupancy.

OccupancyError

Thrown by move_into! when the move would exceed the parent's remaining capacity, per its occupancy/occupancy_cost accounting. See Movement & Occupancy for how occupancy cost is computed.

FixedMembershipError

Thrown when trying to add or remove a slot from a Labware's fixed internal structure, or move a Well independently of its Labware – both are permanently fixed at construction. See Locations for the generic-vs-fixed distinction between GenericLocation and Labware/Well, and Movement & Occupancy for how it applies to move_into!.

MixingError

Thrown by Stock subtraction (-) when the result would leave a reagent at a negative quantity. See Stocks for Stock arithmetic.
